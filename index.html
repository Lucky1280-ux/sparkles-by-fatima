WHAT ARE THE DELIVERY CHARGES?

▼

Our delivery charges are Rs. 299 all over Pakistan.

IS THE PAYMENT ADVANCE?

▼

Yes, we require full advance payment.

If you prefer Cash on Delivery (COD), you will need to pay 50% in advance and the remaining amount on delivery. Please note that COD parcels include 4% government taxes, which is why we recommend full advance payment.

WHEN WILL I GET MY ORDER?

▼

Orders are usually delivered within 3 to 7 working days. We also share a tracking ID so you can track your parcel easily. However, due to unforeseen circumstances in the country, delivery may sometimes be delayed. We kindly ask for your patience in such cases, as delays are beyond our control.

WHAT IS THE RETURN AND EXCHANGE POLICY?

▼

We offer exchanges within 3 days of delivery if the product you received is damaged or incorrect. Please make sure to record an unboxing video as proof so our team can instantly process your request!
